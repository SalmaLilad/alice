A bookmark that keeps your place and your light

Tea-time reading deserves better than a folded corner. The Alice's Book Nook pairs an automatic silicone page tracker with a clear, vertical light that you can slide, recolor, and admire.

Behind the glass, vintage brown gears turn around a tiny clock charm. Flip the page and the checkerboard flipper follows, so your spot is always marked.

Three ways it earns its spot on your shelf

Every part is built to be used one-handed, in bed, with the lights low.

01

Automatic page flipper

A hard clip stays between the pages. A checkerboard silicone piece swings over each time you turn, always resting on the page you are reading.

02

Clear gear housing

The tall, see-through light holds brown vintage gears and a small clock charm. It slides freely along the outer edge of the book.

03

Light and clock orb

Choose any light color with the touch buttons. A mini projector on the back casts a clock onto the page as a glowing orb, and you can switch it off whenever you like.

Notes from the reading nook

What readers get from it, in plain terms.

No more dog-ears

The flipper moves on its own, so you never hunt for your page again.

Light where you need it

Slide the head up or down the edge to put the glow on the lines you are reading.

Mood on demand

Warm amber for cozy chapters, cool blue for midnight mysteries. Touch to switch.
